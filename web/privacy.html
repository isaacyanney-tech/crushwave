Privacy

A plain-language summary of how Crushwave handles your data. A full policy will be published before public launch.

What we collect

Your student email (to prove you’re a student), first name, date of birth (we only show your age), gender and who you want to see, the profile details and photos you add, your likes, matches and chats, and reports you make.

What we never collect

Your exact location, your phone contacts, your surname or your ID card.

Who can see your profile

Only signed-in, verified students from your school. People you block, and people who block you, can never see you. Photos are stored privately and shown through links that expire after an hour. Hidden photo data such as GPS location is removed before upload.

Where your data is stored

On secure servers in the United Kingdom run by our provider, Supabase. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile at any time, and delete your account and all your data from Settings in the app. This follows Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
